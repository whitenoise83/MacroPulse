# Model 1A Known Limitations

- Current target is current-quarter GDP only.
- Intraday release times are not yet modelled.
- The Bridge model uses a no-news carry-forward for unavailable quarterly features.
- The DFM is deliberately parsimonious and may miss sector-specific shocks.
- Nominal retail sales can mix real activity with price changes.
- Forecast intervals are empirical and may react slowly to sudden regime changes.
- Pandemic observations can dominate squared-error metrics.
- Data revisions and methodology changes can alter historical comparability.
- The stable stage map was selected on the development sample and requires live shadow validation.
- The robust adaptive policy remains shadow-only; its switches may still be sensitive to structural breaks.
- The model does not estimate recession probabilities, market returns, or policy
  scenarios; those belong to later MacroPulse models.
