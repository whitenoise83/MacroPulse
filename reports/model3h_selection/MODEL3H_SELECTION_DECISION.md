# Model 3H Selection Decision

## Decision
**3D - State-Space Potential Output** is selected as the governed research candidate to enter Model 3I production hardening.

This is **not** production promotion.

## Predeclared hierarchy
1. Real-time estimability and origin coverage.
2. Numerical and econometric admissibility.
3. Revision and endpoint stability.
4. Economic diagnostic evidence.

No weighted composite score was introduced after observing results.

## Evidence
- 3D: 46/46 converged; 46/46 admissible; endpoint-change MAE 0.233001 pp; RMSE 0.483431 pp.
- 3E-A: 46/46 converged; 8/46 admissible; Phillips sign coherent at 0/46 origins.
- 3E-B: 46/46 converged; 43/46 admissible; endpoint-change MAE 1.054168 pp; RMSE 3.343252 pp; Phillips sign coherent at 27/46 and Okun sign coherent at 46/46 origins.
- 3C benchmarks remain reference diagnostics only.

## Excluded from the decision
The observed-GDP log-level vintage-revision statistic is excluded because benchmark/reference-basis changes can contaminate cross-vintage level comparisons. Model 4 performance, Model 1D prospective-validation status, and cross-system raw likelihood/AIC comparisons are also excluded by governance.

## Authorization
Passing Model 3H authorizes 3D to proceed to **Model 3I - Production Hardening & Release**. It does not mutate Models 1 or 2, retune 3D, or promote anything automatically.
