-------

Behaviour for future runs
-------------------------
When two consecutive runs contain no raw changes and have unchanged component
forecasts and production weights, MacroPulse stores a successful zero-change
comparison with zero contributions and zero residual. Genuine model movement is
not suppressed: any data, component, weight, or production-forecast change
continues through the full attribution engine.
